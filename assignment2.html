Spatial Control Prototypes

My first area of exploration is related to the spatial control of my synthesiser. The spatial input allows users to move a marker around an area to change different characteristics of the sound. I wanted to explore different combinations of controls to see which ones could create interesting sounds while still being simple enough for toddlers to understand through experimentation.

I am concerned with a couple of different aspects of the spatial control. Firstly, approachability, as the controls should be easy to understand without requiring knowledge of technical music or synthesiser terminology. I am also interested in how the different controls can create noticeable changes to the sound and encourage the user to explore the space.

Prototype 1: Filter + Attack

This prototype uses the horizontal position to control the filter, making the sound more muffled on the left and brighter on the right. The vertical position controls the attack, with a short attack at the top and a long attack at the bottom. I wanted to start with these controls because they create noticeable changes to the sound while still giving the user a simple way to explore.

Prototype 2: Pitch + Echo

This prototype changes the controls to pitch and echo. Moving the marker from left to right changes the pitch from low to high, while moving it from top to bottom changes the amount of echo. This prototype is more suited for simpler child minds as it doesn't utilise complex parameter concepts, I wanted to explore these controls because pitch is easy to understand and echo can create more playful and noticeable changes to the sound.

Prototype 3: Four Sound Corners

Finally, this prototype gives each corner of the spatial area a different combination of sound characteristics. For example, one corner could create a soft sound with a low pitch and little echo, while another could create a buzzy sound with a high pitch and lots of echo. The sounds gradually change as the marker moves between the corners, creating different sounds to discover across the space.
